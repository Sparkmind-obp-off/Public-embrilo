# 01 — Market Research and Positioning

## Evidence, not proof of demand
Ipsos Indonesia's 2023 short-video research summary describes short video as an important consumption format; it is dated and not a current market-size estimate: https://www.ipsos.com/en-id/uncovering-growth-short-video-indonesia
A 2026 secondary industry summary describes a fragmented social landscape; verify underlying datasets before using its figures in sales copy: https://beriklan.co.id/riset/laporan-industri-iklan-digital-2026/
Euromonitor's 2025 filmmaking report is broad context, not proof of demand for small content packs: https://www.euromonitor.com/filmmaking-in-indonesia-isic-9211/report

## Positioning candidate
“Brief produkmu. Dapatkan ide, skrip, caption, dan CTA yang siap ditinjau—dibuat dengan bantuan AI dan pemeriksaan manusia.”

## Offers to test
- Short-video script pack.
- Product storytelling pack.
- Weekly content plan.

Package quantities, prices, turnaround and revision rules remain undecided until validated.

## Validation plan
Interview 8–12 target buyers about their last real content task and current spending. Manually fulfil 3–5 pilot jobs. Measure payment, acceptance, time, revisions and repeat orders. Compare the three offers. Do not treat compliments as demand proof.

## Alternatives
DIY AI, freelancers, agencies, templates and in-house creation. Explain Embrilo's structured briefing and human review without claiming competitors are inferior. Never promise viral reach or sales outcomes.
