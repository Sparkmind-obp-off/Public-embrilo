# 00 — Product North Star
## North Star
Move customers from a clear production need to a reviewed, securely delivered result with minimal confusion.

## Product layers
Discover services/examples; compare offers; submit structured brief or quote request; confirm scope/order; pay through verified flow; produce/QC internally; deliver privately; handle eligible revisions; learn from feedback.

## North Star metric
**Accepted Deliverables per Week (ADW)**: orders whose deliverables are accepted or meet a documented completion policy. Read with on-time rate, revision/refund rate, contribution margin, satisfaction, repeat purchase and paid-to-delivery time.

## Principles
Scope before price; price before payment. No unsupported promises, fake testimonials or invented demand. Publish customer work only with explicit documented permission. Keep OS credentials and other customers' data away from browser. Start narrow and manually validate. Separate facts, assumptions, decisions and unknowns.

## MVP non-goals
Multi-vendor marketplace, vendor payouts, customer-facing Genspark access, autonomous production, unverified generation API, subscriptions and public order lookup.

## Promise gate
Publish a promise only when scope, evidence, owner, SOP and failure handling exist.
