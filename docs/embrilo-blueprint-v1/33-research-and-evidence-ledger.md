## Validation record
Question/claim; why it matters; class; source/date; method/sample; findings; confidence/limitations; decision; owner; next review.

Demand evidence should include real conversations, compliant public discussion research, actual briefs, paid pilots, willingness-to-pay and repeat behavior. Likes/views or AI summaries alone do not prove willingness to pay. Record counterevidence and sample limits; never invent TAM/conversion/revenue forecasts.
