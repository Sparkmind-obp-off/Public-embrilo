# 27 — Service Catalog & Offer Specification
Catalog answers what can be ordered and under which scope/price/terms. Showcase demonstrates capability. A service can exist without an example; an example is not automatically purchasable.

## Concepts in previously reviewed code
- Paket skrip video pendek.
- Paket storytelling produk.
- Rencana konten mingguan.
These are service concepts, not proof finalized commercial offers are ready. Price and turnaround were not validated in that review.

## Required fields per offer
ID/slug/version; customer outcome; best-fit use; exact deliverables/quantity/formats; customer inputs; exclusions; revision allowance/definition; timeline/start conditions/dependencies; fixed price or quote-required; currency; cancellation/refund; availability; approved examples; fulfillment checklist; owner and evidence review date.

## Activation checklist
Problem/outcome clear; deliverables countable; formats/delivery defined; inputs and exclusions clear; revision rules; price/quote approved; timeline/capacity realistic; terms/support published; SOP tested; example labelled or omitted; evidence/owner recorded.

Keep pilot offers narrow and manually fulfillable. Measure time, cost, revision load, failure modes and feedback before fixing price.
