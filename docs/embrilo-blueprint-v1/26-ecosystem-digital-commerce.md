# 26 — Ecosystem & Digital Commerce
Offer types: production service; fixed service package; custom engagement; curated digital solution (future, with rights/license/version/support); vendor offer (future, third-party fulfillment). Different rights and fulfillment mean these must not be collapsed into one generic product type.

## Expansion
A: validate service pilots, outcomes, repeatable quality and showcase permission.
B: productize repeatable work only if rights and demand evidence support it.
C: curated digital solutions with clear deliverables, compatibility, license, version, support and updates.
D: consider vendors only after demand/operations mature; add onboarding, terms, commission/payout/tax/refund/dispute, moderation and isolated access.

Canonical offer: ID/version, title/outcome, type, scope/deliverables, price/currency, availability, fulfillment, terms/license, evidence/provenance and assets. Channel pages map to canonical record.

Review platform eligibility and policies before external-channel launch. AI generation alone does not validate demand, rights, quality or economics.
