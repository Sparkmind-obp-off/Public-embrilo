# 03 — Information Architecture and UI/UX

## Experience
Clear, credible, modern and Bahasa Indonesia-first. Explain that AI assists and a human reviews output.

## Landing page sections
1. Hero with clear outcome and “Mulai brief” CTA.
2. Service cards with deliverables and exclusions.
3. Approved examples or clearly labeled demos.
4. Process: brief → production → QC → delivery.
5. Privacy, revision policy and human review.
6. FAQ and final CTA.

## Service details
Explain who it is for, required inputs, exact deliverables, file formats, turnaround only after measured, revision policy after approved, and price only after validation. No guaranteed outcomes.

## Brief fields
Required: service, product description, objective, audience, platform, language, tone, CTA, verified facts, constraints, rights confirmation, AI processing acknowledgment.
Optional: reference URLs, brand voice, campaign date and examples.
Never ask for account passwords, cookies, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive information.

## Form behavior
Group fields into Product, Audience, Content, References and Review. Explain why fields matter. Validate lengths and file types. Success page shows reference and next steps; don't imply production has started until internal acceptance. Provide safe retry if connector is unavailable.

## Status and delivery
Show customer-safe status and timestamps. Hide reviewer notes, prompts and provider logs. Only approved deliverables are downloadable. Revision request appears only when allowed.

## Accessibility
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, adequate contrast, clear errors, responsive layout and Indonesian-first copy.
